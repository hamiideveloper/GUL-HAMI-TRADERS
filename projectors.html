Projector Types — Short Explanation

Office / Business Projectors

Presentations, meetings aur training ke liye. Brightness (lumens) important hota hai taake lights on hone par bhi clear dikhe.

Home / Entertainment

Movies aur home theater ke liye. Contrast aur picture quality zyada important hoti hai.

School / Education

Classrooms ke liye durable aur easy to use projectors. Long lamp life better hoti hai.

Portable / Mini

Chote size, halka weight. Travel ya temporary use ke liye best. Brightness usually kam hoti hai.
